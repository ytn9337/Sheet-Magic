Privacy Policy for Sheet Magic

Last updated: January 17, 2026

Summary: Sheet Magic does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers. Processing happens locally in your browser.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal information, Google Sheets contents, generated files, usage statistics, or browsing history.

2. How Sheet Magic Works

The extension reads data through Google Sheets API, creates PDF/PPT through Google Slides API, and saves files to Google Drive or your computer.

3. Permissions

Permissions are used for Google OAuth, detecting the current sheet, reading page information, saving preferences, and downloading files.

4. Data Storage

Fonts, templates, profiles, schedules, and other settings are stored locally using Chrome storage API.

5. Third-Party Services

Sheet Magic uses Google Sheets, Slides, and Drive APIs. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

Google API communication uses HTTPS. No data is sent to our servers.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Contact: sheetmagic@pocketwork.org

10. Your Rights

You may uninstall the extension, revoke Google API access, and clear locally stored preferences.
